Primary Games Project

Over the course of the year 2026, I was tasked with working in a group to design a game. In this, I worked with four other students. James was the head designer and programmer. Long was the level designer. Andy was the head artist. Raphael was head producer. I designed the music, wrote up the direction for the story, provided some sound effects, and designed the trailer.

Bomb Slime is a single-player 2D side-scrolling action game for Windows PC. You play Inkform, a slime whose body can swallow, carry and spit back out the bombs it finds. It also is in possession of a harpoon, giving it the ability to grapple on to walls and ceilings. It wakes up in an abandoned mine still full of machinery and equipment, and the one bomb it carries is the way through: it sets off the next, opens a wall, or lifts Inkform to where no jump reaches. Inkform will use these tools to make its way through the mines.

Artificial Intelligence Neural Network

In the year 2025, I took a unit titled "Advanced Game Development". For this unit, we were supposed to study a Game Development subject on our own. I based my studies on Artificial Intelligence Neural Networks. For this, I created a 2D platformer course to run through. In this, I put the player at the beginning of the route, placed a series of checkpoints throughout the route, and gave the agent controlling the player the vector towards the checkpoint I wanted it to pass. I would also give it information regarding to the surrounding environment, and place these bits of information in a Neural Network, where the output would be a series of movements. After trial and error, and many improvements to the way I chose which Neural Network save to use, I managed to get my agent to successfully pass no less than 25 checkpoints. For this assignment, I achieved a mark of 71%.

Some day in the future, I intend to make games where Non Playable Characters can be directed around the game environment, navigating it with their own Neural Network. I also want to make the game so that there is a chance that the NPCs don't follow orders.

PERSONAL GAMES PROJECTS

Mechanical Energy Experiment

As early as the year 2017, I have been attempting to make my own magic system similar to that of Avatar, The Last Airbender (2005-2008). One of the forms of this power system is with Mechanical Energy. In the year 2023, I finally started coding in C# how this system should work in a 3D environment

In this system, the player has the ability to absorb the mechanical energy in another object, and to fire it into another object when needed. So if an object is falling to the earth at say a vector with a magnitude of 30 units a second, the player would have the ability to right click on this object, and absorb the mechanical energy, causing the object to come to a stop. The absorbed energy is stored in the player's energy bank. When they right click on the same object or another object, they can fire the object forward with a vector of the magnitude of 10. However, if they have the shift key pressed, they can fire the object with all the energy in their energy bank.

In the future, I am hoping to include things like sound, considering that sound is a form of mechanical energy.

Electromagnetic Energy Experiment

A second form of the power system was with Electromagnetic Energy. As of 2024, I have been attempting to code it into a the same game project. However, I have also made a 2D system, as I feel that it would help me see the direction of the electricity better.

In this system, rather than absorb Mechanical energy, the player can absorb electrons. The system is designed to mirror lightning in real life. When a player wishes to, they can take the electrons from their energy bank, and put them into a charge. They could also create a charge out of negative electrons (so basically, electrons being absorbed by the player creating a positive charge). The Electron charge would have a large field, and a small field. If a negative charge overlaps with a positive charge, the electrons would fire from the negative charge into the positive charge, creating lightning.
